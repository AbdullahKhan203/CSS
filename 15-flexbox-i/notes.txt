

 width:max-content; make whole content in whole line,while width:min-content; keep each word in single line and div with become the width of longest word.

 am 
 PAkistani.

we can't apply hiight and width on inline-element,but when we make inline-element a flex-item,then we will be able to make it height and width.We can give whole properties to flex items,which we give to block elements.
