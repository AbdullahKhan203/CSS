Q:-What is meaing of cross-axes?
Ans:-Great question! Let's break it down clearly:

---

### ✅ What is the **cross axis**?

In **Flexbox**, there are two main axes:

| Axis           | Direction Depends On  | Description                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Main axis**  | `flex-direction`      | The **primary** direction in which items flow. |
| **Cross axis** | Perpendicular to main | The **opposite** (perpendicular) direction.    |

---

### 🔄 Examples:

#### Example 1: `flex-direction: row` (default)

* **Main axis** = horizontal (left to right)
* **Cross axis** = vertical (top to bottom)

#### Example 2: `flex-direction: column`

* **Main axis** = vertical (top to bottom)
* **Cross axis** = horizontal (left to right)

---

### 🧠 So in the line:

> "It centers all the rows or columns as a group in the container's cross axis."

It means:

* If your `flex-direction` is **row** → items wrap into **multiple rows** → `align-content: center` vertically **centers those rows**.
* If your `flex-direction` is **column** → items wrap into **multiple columns** → `align-content: center` horizontally **centers those columns**.

---
