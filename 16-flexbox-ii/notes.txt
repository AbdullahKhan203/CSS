What is diff align-items:base-line and align-items:flex-start?
Ans:-Great question! Both `align-items: baseline` and `align-items: flex-start` are used in **Flexbox** to align flex items along the **cross axis** (which is vertical by default if `flex-direction` is `row`). But they behave differently.
It is applid on parent element.
---

### 🔹 `align-items: flex-start`

* **Meaning**: Aligns all flex items at the **top** of the container (i.e., the **start** of the cross axis).
* **Use Case**: You want all items to align at the top regardless of their content (text, height, etc.).

#### 🔸 Example:

Imagine boxes with different heights:

```
[box1]
[box2     ]
[box3    ]
```

They all start at the **top**.

---

### 🔹 `align-items: baseline`

* **Meaning**: Aligns the **text baseline** of the flex items (typically the line where the text sits).
* **Use Case**: You want all items' **text** to line up properly, even if their heights differ.

#### 🔸 Example:

If items contain text:

```
[Hello ]
[Hi    ]
[Goodbye]
```

Their **text baselines** are aligned, not necessarily their tops or bottoms.

---

### 🧠 Visual Comparison

| Property                  | What aligns?       | Good for                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| `align-items: flex-start` | Top edges of items | Uniform top alignment      |
| `align-items: baseline`   | Text baselines     | Aligning text across items |

---

### 🔍 When to Use What?

* Use **`flex-start`** when layout consistency (e.g., top-aligned cards) is your priority.
* Use **`baseline`** when **text alignment** is more important than box height.
