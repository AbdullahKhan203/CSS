4)fixed:- position fixed work same as position absolute, but it has a bit difference than position absolute, distance is:- like position absolute div connect with his parent that has any position,if his parent has not any position then absolute div connect with viewport, but in case of position fixed, it always connect with viewport,position fixed div does not connect his any parent even his parent has any position,it always connect with viewport.

Remember:- with position absolute or fixed div takes only place  in which his inner content consist,not whole line like block level element.for example:- when we give li position absolute or fixed, then div reach on surface and left the place,which the div take place in his parent,and also decreases his width upto his inner content only except block level element.


5) position sticky work same as position relative,means like position relative div stay in document flow,not reach in surface like fixed and absolute(reach in surface means div goes out of document flow means div go out side of parent size calculation),position sticky work when its parent has fixed height and has scrollbar.position sticky div always stay in his paarent div,not go outside of his parent.If we will not give scrollbar to his position sticky's div, then position sticky work according to viewport.
for example:- in code
```

```
html goes from top 10px margin,but if we not give scroll of his any parent,first it find scroll in his parent,grand parent,grand grand parent,if it dost not find scrll , then it work according to viewport,in case of viewport,if our div goes outside of view port then in that case our position sticky div start working and keep distance of 10px from viewport,but when our whole parent div goes outside from viewport then position sticky div stop keep distance from viewport and pass from viewport because position sticky div always stay in viewport.


Remember:- sticky always work when we make its parent overflow:scroll;if we make its grand parent overflow:scroll; instead of its first parent,it will not work.But if we want to make an element sticky  according to viewport, then we must keep that div in his original parent not nested parent.means if we want to make an element sticky according to view port,then we must keep that sticky element directly in his parent div not nested parent,in which we want the sticky work.It means if we want to make an element view port sticky according to particular div, then we must keep that element in directly in  particular div not in nested form.

Remember: Sticky never skips a non-scrollable parent!
